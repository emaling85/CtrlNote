Capture first, organize later

One hotkey, any app

Ctrl+Alt+N opens a small window on top of whatever you're doing. Ctrl+Enter saves, Esc hides.

Lands in the right folder

Pick a vault folder, or let CtrlNote guess it from the active window.

Voice, offline

Dictate with local Whisper. Your audio never leaves your PC unless you choose OpenAI.

Draw & paste

Sketch on a canvas or paste a screenshot. It's saved as a PNG next to the note.

Plain Markdown

Ordinary .md files with readable names. No database, no lock-in.

English & Russian

Choose the language on first launch, then switch any time in Settings.
